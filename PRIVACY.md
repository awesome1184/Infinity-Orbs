# Infinity Orbs Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 2, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how **Infinity Orbs** (the "App"), developed by **u/awesome1184**, handles information when you use the App on Reddit.

The App operates through Reddit's Developer Platform (Devvit). Your use of Reddit and the App is also subject to Reddit's Privacy Policy, User Agreement, Developer Terms, and other applicable Reddit policies.

## 1. Information We Receive

### Reddit account information

When you use Infinity Orbs while signed in to Reddit, the App receives the Reddit account identifier provided by Devvit so that it can create and load your game account.

The App may also receive your Reddit username. Your username is used to identify you in the game, guilds, guild chat, leaderboards, support requests, and other game features where a username is displayed.

The App does **not** ask you for or store your Reddit password.

### Gameplay information

The App stores information needed to operate your game, which may include:

- Coins, Shards, Cosmic Dust, Orbs, and other in-game resources;
- rolls, rarity and collection information;
- upgrades, mastery, prestige, levels, experience, achievements, quests, streaks, and other progression;
- active effects, boosters, and other game state;
- guild membership, role, contribution, trust status, taxes, perks, challenges, and related guild activity;
- Guild Chat messages and the username associated with those messages;
- timestamps needed for cooldowns, activity tracking, streaks, and other game mechanics;
- share-related game records; and
- purchase records and receipts needed to grant or reverse paid digital goods.

### Payment information

Infinity Orbs uses Reddit's payment system for purchases made through the App.

The App receives transaction information needed to identify and fulfill a purchase, such as a Reddit-provided account identifier, product SKU, order identifier, order status, and purchase/fulfillment information.

Infinity Orbs does not request or store payment card numbers, banking credentials, or Reddit passwords. Payment processing itself is handled through Reddit's payment infrastructure.

### Moderation, security, and diagnostics

The App may store information needed to operate and protect the game, including game bans, moderation actions, guild-chat moderation records, developer/admin actions, and limited technical or payment diagnostics.

Payment diagnostics can include order identifiers, Reddit account identifiers, product SKUs, processing status, and related error or troubleshooting information. These records are used to diagnose payment failures, prevent duplicate fulfillment, investigate abuse, and keep purchases consistent.

The App does not intentionally collect precise location data, contacts, photos, microphone recordings, or device address books.

## 2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

- provide and save your Infinity Orbs game state;
- identify your game account and display your username where game features require it;
- provide guilds, chat, leaderboards, achievements, sharing, quests, and other requested features;
- process and reconcile purchases and digital goods;
- prevent duplicate rewards, fraud, cheating, and abuse;
- enforce game moderation actions;
- diagnose bugs and payment or gameplay problems;
- maintain the security and reliability of the App; and
- comply with Reddit's requirements and other applicable legal obligations.

We do not use the information collected by Infinity Orbs to build advertising profiles or to infer sensitive personal characteristics.

## 3. How Information Is Stored

Infinity Orbs stores game data using Reddit Devvit's managed storage, including installation-scoped Redis storage.

The App's ordinary gameplay data is stored under Reddit-account-linked identifiers so that your game can be restored when you return.

Some records are retained for as long as they are needed for the game's operation. For example, player registry information may be retained so the developer can identify players who have used the game, while short-lived activity records may expire automatically. Guild chat is limited to recent messages, and payment diagnostics are limited to a fixed number of recent records.

Retention periods may change as the App's systems and Reddit's platform requirements change.

## 4. How Information Is Shared

Infinity Orbs does not sell your personal information.

The App may make information visible to other Reddit users when a game feature is designed to do so. For example:

- your Reddit username and relevant game information may appear on leaderboards;
- your username and messages may be visible to members of your guild through Guild Chat;
- guild membership and contribution information may be visible to other guild members or through game features;
- content you explicitly choose to share may be published to Reddit; and
- information required for moderation may be accessible to authorized moderators or the developer.

The App uses Reddit's platform services, including Devvit services and Reddit APIs, to provide the game. Reddit separately processes information under Reddit's own policies.

The App does not sell player information to data brokers or use third-party advertising trackers.

## 5. Reddit and Third-Party Services

Infinity Orbs is built on Reddit's Developer Platform. Reddit provides the account identity, platform infrastructure, payment services, and other services required for the App to function.

The App currently does not intentionally send player data to an external analytics or advertising service.

Reddit may process information about your use of the App under Reddit's own Privacy Policy and terms. Infinity Orbs cannot control data that Reddit independently processes under Reddit's platform policies.

## 6. Your Choices and Data Requests

You can stop using Infinity Orbs at any time.

For questions about or requests concerning Infinity Orbs data, contact **u/awesome1184** through Reddit and describe the account or game information involved. Requests may be subject to verification, Reddit's requirements, and applicable law.

Some information may need to be retained when required for security, fraud prevention, payment reconciliation, dispute handling, legal obligations, or Reddit platform requirements.

Because the App is tied to a Reddit account, deleting or changing your Reddit account can affect your ability to access your game data. Reddit separately controls account-level data handled by Reddit.

## 7. Security

We use reasonable measures available through Devvit to protect stored game information and restrict sensitive administrative and payment functionality.

No online service can guarantee absolute security. Humanity has unfortunately not yet invented the magical database that cannot ever break.

If we become aware of a security incident affecting information controlled by the App, we will take reasonable steps to investigate and respond, including actions required by Reddit's policies or applicable law.
